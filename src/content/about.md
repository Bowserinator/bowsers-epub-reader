A small, simple epub reader by Bowserinator. Works offline; everything is on your computer.

## Features

- Opens 1 epub (either from empty state or command line)
- Highlights, bookmarks, reading progress saved per book (by SHA-256 hash)
- Full text search (with optional regex)
- View epub metadata (click title in header)
- (per-book or global): Themes, fonts, adjust margin, line height, font size, alignment, page layout, chapter headings, page numbers, etc...
- Estimated time to complete chapter (with adjustable reading speed)
- Draggable progress bar, keyboard shortcuts, scroll, swipe gestures to page
- Hover to view word definitions from StarDict dictonaries
